This privacy policy applies only to the Weave 2.0 Wear OS watch face published by ZWV Labs (“Service Provider”). It does not cover other ZWV Labs applications such as SpaceJelly, which have their own privacy policies.

About Weave 2.0

Weave 2.0 is a Watch Face Format (WFF) watch face. It is a resource-only application: it does not include custom app logic that collects, stores, or transmits personal data to ZWV Labs.

Data collection

Weave 2.0 does not collect personal information. We do not gather account data, location, contacts, analytics events, advertising identifiers, or crash reports through this watch face.

Complications and system data

Optional complication slots (for example battery, steps, weather, or calendar) display information provided by Wear OS and other apps you already use. That data stays on your device and is rendered by the system watch-face runtime. Weave 2.0 does not send it to ZWV Labs or any third-party server operated by us.

Permissions and network

Weave 2.0 does not request custom permissions for its own operation and does not require network access to function as a watch face.

Children

Weave 2.0 is a general-audience personalization product. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
